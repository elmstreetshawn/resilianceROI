# Submission write-up (150–300 words)

**Problem.** Base Power's live signup funnel asks generic, evidence-free screening
questions ("what's your main reason for considering Base?"), asks the same thing twice
in different words, and sends every visitor into the same sales queue regardless of
whether a battery actually makes sense at their address. The result: noisy, unqualified
leads and wasted sales-team time on customers a battery doesn't help.

**Who it helps.** Base's sales and lead-qualification team, who currently can't tell a
warm lead from noise before it reaches them — and the homeowners themselves, who get
generic marketing copy instead of an answer specific to their own grid.

**Solution.** ResilianceROI replaces guesswork with a real, address-specific
severe-weather/grid-risk index built from ERCOT outage reports, NOAA Storm Events, and
EIA-861 utility reliability filings — shown to the customer with a full "how we
calculated this" breakdown, not a black-box score. Qualification uses a four-square
value framing (rate savings, backup hours, outage cost avoided, rate certainty) instead
of ROI/payback math, since the battery is a subscription add-on, never purchased. The
funnel then carries a qualified lead through a photo-based site survey — a local vision
model (Ollama + Qwen2.5-VL, no API key, nothing leaves the machine) gives real-time,
actionable guidance on the photos themselves and suggests where the battery goes in the
customer's own photo — real municipal permitting lookup, and scheduling, instead of
dropping them at "email us."

**Impact.** A funnel that qualifies itself before it ever reaches a human, using real
data and real local AI at every step instead of assumptions or a purchased API — so
Base's sales team spends time on customers a battery genuinely helps, and homeowners get
an honest answer instead of a sales pitch.
